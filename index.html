About Our Mortgage Calculator

Our free mortgage calculator helps you estimate your monthly mortgage payments and understand the complete cost of your loan. Input your loan amount, interest rate, and term to see a detailed breakdown of your payments and potential savings with extra payments.

Key Features:

  • Calculate monthly mortgage payments
  • View detailed amortization schedule
  • See the impact of extra payments
  • Calculate total interest savings
  • Compare different loan scenarios

How to Use:

  1. Enter your loan amount
  2. Input the annual interest rate
  3. Specify the loan term in years
  4. Optionally add extra monthly payments
  5. Click "Calculate" to see your results

Understanding your mortgage payments is crucial for making informed decisions about your home purchase. Our calculator provides accurate calculations and helps you visualize how extra payments can reduce your loan term and save you money in interest.
